Independent Project

AR CAD Viewer

A project focused on making CAD models easier to visualize and interact with using augmented reality.

CAD VIEWER SCREENSHOT / PROJECT PHOTO

The Idea

While working with CAD models, I became interested in how difficult it can be to understand the size and physical relationships of parts when looking at a model on a screen. I wanted to explore whether augmented reality could make CAD models easier to visualize.

Development

I worked on creating a system that could display a CAD model in an augmented environment. The project required thinking about both the engineering model itself and how a user would interact with it.

This project gave me experience working outside of robotics and applying CAD and engineering concepts to a software-based project.

What I Learned

The project showed me that engineering design is not only about creating a physical object. The way a design is visualized and communicated can be just as important.

It also gave me experience combining mechanical design, CAD, and programming concepts into one project.
