VEX Robotics

VEX Robotics gave me a smaller-scale environment where I could work closely with teammates through the complete process of designing, building, programming, and competing with a robot.

VEX ROBOT PHOTO

Team Dynamite

I served as a Design Lead for our VEX team. Our robot, Mr. D, was designed around collecting game objects, transferring them through a conveyor system, scoring, and parking.

Our team competed at the regional level, finishing 3rd out of 40 teams and earning an Excellence Award.
