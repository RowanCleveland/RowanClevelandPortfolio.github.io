VEX Robotics/RECF

VEX Robotics provided me with a smaller-scale environment where I could work closely with teammates through the process of designing, building, programming, and competing with a robot.

Team Dynamite

Team Dynamite was initially run only by an 8th grade student hand his mother, and he needed extra support to keep up with the other teams who had many more kids. I joined, and immediately I loved the smaller-scale robots I was able to work on and the opportunity it gave me to be involved with every step of the design process.

Our team competed at the regional level last year, and has already qualified for state competition this year after only our first competition. Our awards while I have been apart of the team consist of: - The Excellence Award - The Inspire Award (state) - Skills Award - Tournament Champions Award

Competition

Competing with the robot showed me how mechanical design, programming, driving, and teamwork all affect the final performance of a robot.

It also gave me another opportunity to test designs in a real competition environment and identify what could be improved for the next iteration. It also gave me the chance to improve my teaching skills, grow as a teamate, develop my mechanical knowledge, and get to know new amazing people.
