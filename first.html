FIRST Robotics

My sophomore year, I joined the T3 FIRST Robotics Team at Tomball High School where I have spent the last three years building and designing robots. Robotics has me both increased my interest in engineering and helped me to better my mechanical and problem-solving skills.

Design Leadership

As the design lead on T3, I have led design meetings, trained new students hoping to design the robot in the future, grown the design team from four students to 20, and helped design the actual robots for our team's competitions.

As Design Lead, I worked with students to help them learn the design process and learn how to use CAD to create models and robots.

What I Worked On

Robot Design

Designed mechanisms and assemblies while working within the physical and competition constraints of the robot.

Prototyping

Built and tested physical prototypes before committing to final mechanisms.

Design Process

Compared possible solutions and used testing and iteration to improve designs.

Human Player

Played as human player for last season's competitions, shooting the balls into our teams active hub to score.

Featured Design
