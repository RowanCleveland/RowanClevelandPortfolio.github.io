FIRST Robotics

My experience as a Design Lead on a FIRST Robotics Competition team has been one of my biggest introductions to engineering as a collaborative process.

Design Leadership

I became involved in the team's mechanical design process and learned CAD extensively in order to contribute to the robot's development.

As Design Lead, I worked with students across the design process while helping newer members learn CAD and understand how mechanical decisions affect the rest of the robot.

What I Worked On

CAD

Designed mechanisms and assemblies while working within the physical and competition constraints of the robot.

Prototyping

Built and tested physical concepts before committing to final mechanisms.

Design Process

Compared possible solutions and used testing and iteration to improve designs.
